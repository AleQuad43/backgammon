Two devices

Open this page on your phone and on the computer, and pick a different side on each: one plays you, the other plays Luca. Moves, throws and doubles show up on the other screen as they happen. Each screen shows its own player at the bottom, in ivory.

Try

  • Throw your dice: tap anywhere on the board, or swipe from anywhere. They can roll over both halves; once they stop they slide to your side. Let go anywhere on the board, edge included; letting go off the board throws nothing.
  • Tap a checker: it moves by the higher die. Tap the dice to swap which one goes first.
  • Changed your mind? Drag a moved checker back to where it started: its point lights in the second sky colour, and the move is undone. If it used both dice, you can also drop it on the point where it stopped in between: only the second die is undone. Or double tap it to send it straight back.
  • Try a bear-off position: all your checkers are home and it's your throw, with Luca still bringing his last ones in.
  • When you have no dice left to play, press and hold an empty part of the board (a ring fills up under your finger), then slide right: the sky makes a full turn with your finger and the turn ends when it comes back round.
  • Drag a checker: a point lights up only when you can land there, with one die or both. Points just next to a good one get a dull warning; dropping there pulls the checker back onto the good point.
  • To double: before you roll, drag the red cube from the rail onto the board. It turns over as it goes in; once it is fully inside the playing area it shows ×2, and letting go offers the double.
  • When Luca doubles you, the sky turns to your side and a big ×2 sits where your dice go. Tap it to take, or hold and slide to give up the game.
  • End your turn and watch the sky: the three sunset colours swing half a turn around the screen, slow, fast, slow. They swing on again when it comes back to you.
